The shared `SessionStart` hook timeout is 30 s (Grok's own default; Claude's is 60 s), up from 10 s. A live Grok 1.0.13 Windows probe on 2026-09-07 recorded its session_start hook at 14.8 s and 18.2 s (`hook_execution` elapsed_ms) where the same payload runs in under a second when called directly and a cold `pwsh` alone costs 3.3 s here; at 10 s the hook was killed and the continuity brief never parked. Grok spawns a shell per hook call, so its PreToolUse floor is that shell's start; the `args` manifest form that would avoid the shell is a probe on record. Obligation 9861.

- Anchored-metric citation contracts (E9, U-T3): `register_metric_contract`/`godmode metric-contract register --name <name> --anchor <regex>` declares the one output shape a numeric claim about `<name>` may cite, stored as a `decision` record under `metric-contract:<name>`; the anchor is validated at registration (`re.compile` plus a 200-character cap - the E49-absorbed unsafe-pattern idea, sufficient at this scale rather than a bespoke backtracking detector). `_citation_resolves` gains a `line:<name>:<value>` citation kind, resolving only when a contract is registered for `<name>` and the reconstructed `"<name>:<value>"` text matches its anchor. `record_claim` cross-checks any registered metric name appearing in the (markdown-emphasis-stripped) claim text against the first number in that text: a `line:` citation whose value disagrees downgrades naming both numbers ("the cited line says X, the claim says Y"); an unregistered metric name gets no friction from this at all.

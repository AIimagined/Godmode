- `godmode doctor --repair-fork` repairs an archive left with two records at the same sequence at its tip. It keeps the record the chain anchor names (the earliest one when no anchor names either), moves the others into a quarantine folder beside the archive's records rather than deleting them, records the repair as its own entry, and re-verifies the chain. Any other shape - a duplicate below the tip, siblings that do not share one predecessor, a record that fails its own hash - is refused and left untouched.

- An `ask_only` policy no longer turns an R2/R3 ask into an allow while the session is unattended (`GODMODE_ATTENDED=0`, `CI`, or a no-human-ask permission mode). The call follows the unattended rules every other path follows: it asks where a person can answer, and is refused where the host answers its own asks. Attended sessions keep the focused posture unchanged.
- Standing lessons are stricter for an actor nobody is watching. Unattended, a standing lesson cannot be rewritten, retired, amended or expunged; attended, the change proceeds and its diff is shown. `skill retire` archives rather than deletes, and the new `skill restore --seq <retirement seq>` puts a retired skill back.

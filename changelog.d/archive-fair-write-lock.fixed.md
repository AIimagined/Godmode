- Writers under heavy load are no longer refused with "archive is busy". The archive's write lock used to be won by whichever waiting writer happened to retry first, so with many hooks writing at once a writer could lose every retry until its deadline passed. Writers now take a place in line and enter the lock in arrival order, so each waits only for the writers ahead of it; a writer that dies in line is skipped once its process is gone, and a live writer that holds the lock is still waited on, never overtaken.

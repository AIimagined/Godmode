# Godmode Forge Contract

Approve a proposal only when every answer is yes:

- Does local evidence show the same missing capability in at least two real uses?
- Did inspection rule out an adequate installed or project skill?
- Is the responsibility narrow enough to explain in one sentence?
- Are there at least two positive and two confusingly similar negative routing cases?
- Are the acceptance assertions directly observable rather than stylistic claims?
- Is a baseline preserved for comparison?
- Is generated content original and free of external source references?
- Can the skill operate without a daemon, model subprocess, telemetry, or implicit network access?
- Have overlapping proposals been collapsed into one compatible workflow?
- Do deterministic structure validation and behavioral evaluation both pass?

Reject or defer the proposal when any answer is no. A task can still be completed directly without becoming a permanent skill.

# Purpose

This skill exists so spend is checked against declared ceilings from local
records before more is spent, instead of being discovered at the end of a
session or a release as a number nobody decided.

## Gap evidence

- One release ran the full pre-push suite five times for one HEAD and took
  nine password rounds before anything recorded what that cost.
- Session spend was quoted from memory and from estimates; the measured
  series with its gaps was never the thing read.
- Ceilings were declared in a file and checked by nobody until a session
  had already passed them.

## Promise

Spend is read from the record, checked against the ceilings, shown as a
series with its gaps, folded beside gate activity per session and per
release, and answered with a recorded decision.

Future edits to this skill append their own plain-language evidence above.

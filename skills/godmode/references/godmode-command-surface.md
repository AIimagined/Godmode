# Godmode Command Surface

Invoke commands as `python <plugin>/scripts/godmode.py --project <root> <command>`.

- `init`, `inspect`, `resume`, `context status|rebuild|why`, `inventory diff`, and
  `history` establish and reconstruct local reality.
- `plan`, `build`, `checkpoint`, `checklist update`, and `remember` preserve private
  intent, progress, evidence, decisions, invariants, lessons, and obligations.
- `doctor`, `privacy`, `report`, `export`, and `explain-context` verify or explain the
  archive without raw source or transcript export.
- `guard`, `authorize setup|issue`, and `actions` preview protected work and manage
  exact, expiring, one-use capabilities. They never execute the operation.
- `branches`, `version`, `db`, `sprint`, and `docs` record domain-specific governance.
- `parity --reference <local-directory>` compares neutral local structure. URLs are
  refused and content is not copied.
- `skill validate|forge` verifies or creates an original project skill.

Use `--help` on a command for its exact arguments. Completion statuses require evidence.

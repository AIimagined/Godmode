# Purpose

This skill exists so a claim's citations are shaped to grade the first time
- exit-bearing commands through `--verify`, a falsifier on every hypothesis,
stale evidence repaired from the record - instead of being argued with the
grader through wrapper scripts and repeated Stop-gate blocks.

## Gap evidence

- seq:16712 - "Plan 3 Task 7: exit-bearing cite forms verify without
  wrapper scripts; state-reporting and filter tails cap at observed": the
  record that introduced the exit-code-aware `claim --verify` grading this
  skill's step 3 fronts. Before it, wrapper scripts were written only to
  satisfy the cite grader.
- seq:16515 - "Plan 3 Task 5: falsifiers age into a preflight finding after
  two days; verify --falsifiers runs the due ones (d773a2b)": the record that
  introduced `godmode verify --falsifiers`, which this skill's step 5 fronts.
- seq:10319 - the obligation closed as "built 2026-09-09: evidence_versions
  on claims, stale_claims sweep, claim --stale": the record that introduced
  `godmode claim --stale`, which this skill's step 2 fronts.

## Promise

A claim is reflected against the record, cited in a shape that verifies,
given a falsifier when it is a hypothesis, repaired when its evidence
drifts, and resolved once with fresh evidence.

Future edits to this skill append their own seq: citation above.

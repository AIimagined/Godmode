# Purpose

This skill exists so a claim's citations are shaped to grade the first time
- exit-bearing commands through `--verify`, a falsifier on every hypothesis,
stale evidence repaired from the record - instead of being argued with the
grader through wrapper scripts and repeated Stop-gate blocks.

## Gap evidence

- Before exit-code-aware citation grading existed, wrapper scripts were
  written for no reason other than to satisfy the citation grader.
- A hypothesis could sit unfalsified indefinitely, with nothing to age it
  into a due finding and rerun it automatically.
- A claim's supporting evidence could go stale with nothing tracking its
  age or sweeping it up for a recheck.

## Promise

A claim is reflected against the record, cited in a shape that verifies,
given a falsifier when it is a hypothesis, repaired when its evidence
drifts, and resolved once with fresh evidence.

Future edits to this skill append their own plain-language evidence above.

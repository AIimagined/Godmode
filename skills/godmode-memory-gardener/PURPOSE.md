# Purpose

This skill exists so the archive a later session inherits is pruned by
rule - previewed expiry, superseded duplicates, promotions a second actor
approved - instead of growing until the brief carries stale and
contradicting records side by side.

## Gap evidence

- A forgetting mechanism with a dry-run preview and a cold storage tier
  existed, but nothing yet ran it as one pass alongside the
  duplicate-and-contradiction list and the promotion step beside it.
- A way to promote a lesson to the project's law and a way to approve that
  promotion both existed, but a lesson could still reach the law without an
  independent approval tying the two together.

## Promise

At session close the session is told as dated prose, duplicates and
contradictions are listed and superseded on the record, expiry is previewed
before it runs, and a candidate cluster graduates only through a cited
promotion and an independent approval.

Future edits to this skill append their own plain-language evidence above.

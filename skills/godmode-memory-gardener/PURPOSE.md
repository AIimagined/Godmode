rule - previewed expiry, superseded duplicates, promotions a second actor
approved - instead of growing until the brief carries stale and
contradicting records side by side.

## Promise

At session close the session is told as dated prose, duplicates and
contradictions are listed and superseded on the record, expiry is previewed
before it runs, and a candidate cluster graduates only through a cited
promotion and an independent approval.

Future edits to this skill append their own seq: citation above.

# Godmode v0.3.10

The potency release: every silence found by the same-day audit gets its remedy.
Receipt-bounded advisories now work on hosts that send only a transcript path;
the brief detects the multiple-writable-truths disease (many status-shaped
markdown files) and names the cure; `status absorb-docs` opens a door through
the adoption wall by mapping a status-shaped markdown file into proposed store
items - dry-run first, checked boxes absorbed as claims-of-done, never as
verified. Doctor reads the archive once; the context cap truncates inventory,
never the commands; stop notices cap at two; review-shaped and done-check
prompts deliver their verbs; the sibling collapse states its muted count; a
non-HARD enforcement grade states itself on the brief.

The loop family unparks: `godmode loop declare/tick/close` records bounded loop
contracts beside the existing repetition detectors - no cap or stop condition
refuses to declare, two empty iterations demand a direction change and four
demand the operator, and closing `finished` at the iteration cap requires
evidence, because budget exhaustion may never impersonate completion.

Also: declarative per-tool gates (`tool_gates` in the authorization policy,
tighten-only, loud on loosening values), a plain-text `hooks statusline`
segment carrying presence and the live enforcement grade, and a token-cost
footer on the emitted AGENTS.md section.

## Verifying

- `python -m unittest discover -s tests` — the full suite, or the four-chunk
  form via `scripts/dev/run_with_flaky_retry.py`.
- `godmode loop declare demo --max-iterations 3 --stop-when "done"` then two
  `loop tick demo --empty` calls — the second escalates.
- `godmode status absorb-docs <file>` on any checkbox markdown — the mapping
  prints, nothing writes without `--write`.
- `godmode hooks statusline` — the segment; the grade matches `hooks status`.
